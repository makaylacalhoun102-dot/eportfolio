The concept

Design with purpose.

A community wellness campaign designed to encourage emotional honesty and self-kindness. The message “It’s okay to not be okay” is paired with a soft sage-green palette, dark green typography, and a friendly peach illustration to make the design feel approachable and supportive. This is a fictional design concept, not a clinical service.
