MAISON SOLÉ

Boutique hotel brand identity · Canva

The concept

Design with purpose.

A cohesive identity concept for a fictional boutique hotel. The project uses a burgundy, cream, and gold palette, a refined MS monogram, and consistent typography across a room key, guest welcome letter, envelope, and promotional design. The goal was to create a warm, elegant guest experience across different touchpoints.
